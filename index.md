1. Information We Collect
1.1 Photos You Provide
When you use the App, you may upload or capture photos of yourself (or others, with their consent) to receive AI-generated hairstyle suggestions. These photos may contain facial imagery, which is treated as sensitive personal data.

1.2 Account Information
If the App requires an account, we may collect your name, email address, and authentication credentials.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:

Generate AI-powered hairstyle suggestions and image modifications
Send your photo to our third-party AI processing provider (OpenAI) to perform the requested image generation/modification
Improve and maintain the App's functionality
Respond to customer support requests
Monitor for misuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service
Comply with legal obligations
3. How Your Photos Are Processed (Third-Party AI Disclosure)
To provide hairstyle suggestions, photos you upload are transmitted to OpenAI (via the OpenAI API/SDK) for processing and image modification.

OpenAI processes this data as a service provider on our behalf, under their own Privacy Policy and API data usage policies.
Data submitted through the OpenAI API is not used by OpenAI to train their models, per OpenAI's API terms, which may be updated from time to time.
OpenAI may retain API inputs/outputs for a limited period (commonly up to 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring, after which it is deleted, unless retention is required by law.
We recommend you review OpenAI's own privacy policy for full details on how they handle data submitted via their API.

4. Data Storage and Retention
Uploaded photos: We give users the option to store their photos indefinitely until they delete them or delete their account.
Account data: Retained for as long as your account is active.
5. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

OpenAI — for AI-based image processing, as described above.
Legal authorities — if required by law, or to protect our legal rights, users, or the public.

You can delete individual photos or your entire account at any time from within the App. Deleting your account removes your stored photos and personal data from our systems, except where we are required to retain it by law.

If you have questions about your data or need help with deletion, contact us at gurungbeejaya@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law (e.g. within 30 days under GDPR/CCPA).

If you are located outside the United States, note that your photos and data are processed in the United States (by us and by OpenAI), where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

7. Data Security
We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls on stored images. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

By uploading a photo, you confirm that it is a photo of yourself, or that you have obtained consent from the person shown to upload and process their image. You may not upload photos of individuals without their consent, including minors.
